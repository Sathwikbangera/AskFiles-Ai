Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full-time remote work requires written approval from a department director.

Equipment: The company provides a laptop and a one-time $200 home office
stipend for remote employees. Requests for additional equipment must be
submitted through the IT portal.

Working hours: Remote employees must be available during core hours,
9:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time, and must attend all scheduled team meetings.

Security: Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal
systems and must not use public Wi-Fi for work involving confidential data.
